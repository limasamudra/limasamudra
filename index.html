Indonesia's Trusted Dried & Frozen Seafood Exporter — Lima Samudra

Lima Samudra is a professional Indonesian dried and frozen seafood supplier and exporter based in Surabaya, East Java, Indonesia. We specialise in bulk export of premium marine products including dried sea cucumber (teripang), shark fin (sirip hiu), fish maw (gelembung ikan), shark bone, shark lips, shark skin, dried beef tendon (otot sapi kering), jellyfish (ubur-ubur), and frozen pigeon (merpati beku).

We serve importers, distributors, restaurants, and specialty food buyers across Asia and worldwide. Our products are available in multiple grades, in both dried and frozen formats where applicable. Contact us via WhatsApp at +62-851-9006-1790 or WeChat christofsusanto for pricing and samples.
